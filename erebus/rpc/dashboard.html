Reports

Generate an engagement report from the current session's findings, hosts and evidence.

New Engagement

Pick a platform, paste or load the program's scope, and Erebus sets the deny-all gate (in-scope allowed, out-of-scope excluded), attaches the platform's rules of engagement, and records them in the report. The program's own brief/policy always supersedes these - this is a pre-flight aid, not legal advice.

Subdomain Enum

Passive multi-source subdomain discovery — crt.sh, HackerTarget, AlienVault OTX and Anubis, merged and de-duped. Every query hits a public OSINT dataset, never the target. Discovered hosts land in the graph; with an engagement set up, out-of-scope ones are flagged. Leave sources blank for all four.

Notes

A living engagement notepad - findings, next steps, loose ends. Saved to disk and persists across restarts.
